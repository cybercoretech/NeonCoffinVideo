## What the graphics contain

- Die-cut coffin: new detailed adaptation of the approved cassette badge, with a closed coffin outline, opaque dark interior and clear exterior.
- Die-cut marquee: a new straight-on adaptation of the approved storefront sign, retaining jagged pink horror lettering, green VIDEO lettering and the skull.
- Vinyl decals: the complete original storefront scene, plus the new marquee. The scene includes the drive-in, moon, cars and rain reflections; it was not regenerated.
- Holographic coffin: a separate illustration with broad bright highlights. Actual holographic shimmer comes from Fourthwall's material. The file does not contain a simulated rainbow finish.
- Collector sheet: four separate stickers — marquee, coffin cassette, full storefront scene and the existing pink/green `NeonCoffinVideo.site` wordmark on an opaque dark plaque. Gaps between stickers are truly transparent. The sheet has no baked-in background or guide lines that could join all four stickers into one cut shape.

## Verified export facts

All eight PNGs were checked at their actual pixel dimensions, visually inspected, and saved with 300 pixels/inch metadata and an embedded sRGB ICC profile. The sheet is exactly 1749 x 2481 pixels. Individual files have one connected shape and no transparent holes inside; the sheet has four connected shapes. Sheet artwork islands have at least 150 pixels of bounding-box clearance (0.5 inch at 300 PPI), leaving room for production borders.

Original generated drawings are preserved in `source-generated/`. Editable SVG print layouts are in `editable-layouts/`. These SVGs embed raster illustrations; they are not fully vector illustrations and do not add unlimited detail. Layout export was performed with librsvg/ImageMagick; no original approved source files were overwritten.

The individual artwork canvases describe their maximum dimensions, not a promise that Fourthwall's final cut will measure exactly that size. Preserve proportions; do not stretch a coffin or marquee into a square. Check the cut outline and displayed artwork size in the actual product designer. Fourthwall adds its own white production border on standard white vinyl. PNG transparency defines the silhouette; it does not make the finished vinyl clear.

For the landscape scene/decal, confirm the product designer's orientation controls before fitting it to a portrait product template. The file is 4 x 2.25 inches at 300 PPI; do not crop the scene or stretch it to 3 x 4 inches.

The holographic catalog currently lists 3 x 3, 4 x 4 and 5.5 x 5.5-inch sizes. Fourthwall's general sticker help article instead describes an A5 holographic sheet. These conflict: use the dimensions shown by the selected product's actual designer. The holographic artwork supplied here is a single coffin, not a prearranged multi-sticker sheet. Their help article says white highlights reveal the holographic material; exact appearance is a material/printing property, not established by this flat artwork preview.

These are verified artwork files, not a verification of Fourthwall's production cut proof or finished print. Review its generated cut boundaries before adding each item to the shop.
