# First NCV YouTube Short — free launch plan

Title: Miss the video store? I built one for horror fans.
Format: 1080 × 1920, approximately 30 seconds, captions, original NCV graphics and your own voice. Reuse the finished original video as a Facebook Reel. No paid ads needed to start. Publication and channel creation are separate owner actions.

## Voiceover

“Remember when choosing a horror movie meant wandering a video store? I built Neon Coffin Video for that feeling. Cult horror, practical-effects monsters, Friday double features, and links to watch on official services. This week: The Mutilator and Prom Night. Grab a tape, check what’s available in your region, and join us in the Midnight Lounge. Visit NeonCoffinVideo dot site—the link’s on my channel profile. Bad movies. Great nights.”

The featured pair is issue 001, October 9, 2026. Update the pair to match the current Friday issue if publishing later.

## Upload copy

Description: “The video-store feeling is back. Neon Coffin Video is my after-hours corner for cult horror, Friday double bills, movie talk, and links to official viewing services. Visit neoncoffinvideo.site through the link on my channel profile. Availability varies by region. Independent site; not affiliated with Tubi or the studios.”

Hashtags: #CultHorror #VHS #HorrorMovies

Put https://neoncoffinvideo.site/screening-room in the YouTube channel profile links. Shorts descriptions/comments do not provide clickable external URLs. If you later upload a longer NCV introduction, use the Short's Related video feature to connect to it; eligible long-form video descriptions can carry external links with advanced features enabled. Official guidance: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en-uk

## First week

1. Publish the introduction Short and the same original video as a Facebook Reel.
2. Two days later, post a specific 20–30 second recommendation: “A forgotten slasher for Friday night.” Use your own voice and NCV artwork.
3. On Friday, publish the week's double-bill teaser and post the Dispatch edition through Google Groups. End with an easy question people can answer.
4. Reply to actual comments and direct interested viewers to the site/profile link. Share in horror groups only where the rules permit relevant creator links; lead with a useful movie recommendation.

Use Search Console to track search impressions/clicks, YouTube Analytics for the Shorts, and Google Groups for actual opt-in membership. The site's anonymous view counter is not a unique-visitor or campaign analytics system. Do not infer visitor totals or conversions from video views.
