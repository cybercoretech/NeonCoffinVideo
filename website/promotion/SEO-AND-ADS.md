## AdSense

AdSense monetizes existing website visits; it is not a traffic-acquisition tool. Google Ads is the separate paid advertising product. Recommendation: leave AdSense off during the initial audience build and focus on repeat visitors, useful editorial content, the Dispatch, and the already-live merch/support links. This is an editorial/product recommendation, not a claim about a guaranteed minimum traffic threshold or expected earnings.

AdSense can be evaluated later, subject to account/site approval and Google's publisher policies. Horror subject matter alone is not an automatic verdict. Graphic imagery, graphic descriptions, prominent profanity, and certain sexual content can limit or prohibit monetization. The 18+ label does not establish ad eligibility. An open discussion board also requires ongoing content moderation for any monetized inventory. No AdSense script, auto ads, tracking/consent platform, ads.txt publisher ID, or account was added.
