THE BACK ROOM / OLD ORCHARD BEACH, MAINE

THE VIEWING ROOM.

Past the returns slot and the beaded curtain there is a room the store doesn’t advertise. One sagging green sofa. One 1950s console TV with a Betamax deck wired straight into its back, no remote, no input switch. A shelf of tapes nobody files.

Customers are welcome to use it. Most of them watch something off the shelf and go home. A few pick the other tape.

📼 BEHIND THE COUNTER

THE NIGHT MANAGER

“Room’s in the back, past the returns. Sofa’s seen bettah days. So’s the TV. Go on, kid. Take your time.”

He has worked the late shift longer than the store has had a sign. He knows every tape on that shelf, and he knows which one you’re going to pick before you do.

🎞️ THE SHELF NOBODY FILES

PICK A TAPE.

One tape on the shelf is a Betamax. The deck behind the TV is the only thing in the store that will play it.

The Beta deck takes the tape with a heavy clunk. The TV hums. The picture rolls once and waits.

The Viewing Room and everything that happens in it are Neon Coffin Video fiction.
