HOUSE RULES

Come for the movies. Stay kind to the people.

This is an adults-only horror lounge. Swearing, dark humor, and strong opinions about a movie are welcome. Keep the heat on the film, not on each other.

LOUNGE RATINGRNC-1718+MAXAdults only · community guide, not official film classifications
01

Be cool to each other

No harassment, hate speech, slurs, threats, doxxing, or repeatedly targeting someone. Disagree freely; do not try to make another member feel unsafe or unwelcome.

02

Talk about the tape

Profanity and blunt movie criticism are fine. Personal abuse, humiliation, and nasty comments aimed at another member cross the line.

03

Keep the room adult

Members must be 18 or older. Mature horror topics are welcome; sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate material, and graphic real-world harm are not.

04

Give spoilers a warning

Mark major reveals and endings so everyone can choose when to open the coffin.

05

Respect privacy and the space

Do not share someone else’s private information. Skip spam, scams, impersonation, and links to pirated movies.

06

Use the report button, then move on

If something feels out of line, report the post for review. Do not dogpile or turn a disagreement into a campaign against someone.
