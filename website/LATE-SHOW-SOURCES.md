## Original NCV material

The television cabinet (`assets/late-show-tv.png`) is existing original NCV artwork. - [Bloody Disgusting coverage](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3964147/tubi-launches-permanent-terror-on-tubi-horror-hub-with-13000-hours-of-scares/) inspired this discovery section; its article, screenshot, and promotional design were not reproduced.
- [Tubi terms](https://legal-asset.tubi.tv/terms-of-use.html) apply to service use. This new section uses manually maintained outbound public title links. It does not extract streams, frame full films, bypass ads or regional restrictions, or establish a catalog scraping process. No API/player license or Tubi partnership has been obtained. A disclaimer does not supply permission; seek approval before expanding into Tubi content feeds, branding, or full-film embedding.

Title listings checked: [The Mutilator (1984)](https://tubitv.com/movies/505052/the-mutilator), [Hellraiser (1987)](https://tubitv.com/movies/478798/hellraiser), [Event Horizon (1997)](https://tubitv.com/movies/672124/event-horizon), [Prom Night (1980)](https://tubitv.com/movies/449545/prom-night). Their buttons say “Check on Tubi.” Listings do not guarantee playback in a visitor's region and can change. All new rental artwork is NCV art, not art downloaded from those listings.

Uploader identification does not guarantee that every video remains embeddable or grant reuse rights outside the official player. ## Newsletter

The Midnight Dispatch joins through [the Google Group](https://groups.google.com/g/neon-coffin-midnight-dispatch). Google manages membership and email delivery. NCV does not collect email addresses in this signup form. Joining requires completing Google's flow; clicking the NCV button does not subscribe a visitor. Members choose “Each email” or another delivery frequency and can leave in Google Groups. Public issues and RSS require no subscription.

Group settings are controlled by the owner and have not been independently verified. See [Google's group settings reference](https://support.google.com/groups/answer/2464926?hl=en). The scheduled NCV archive does not send emails automatically; the owner publishes each email edition in Google Groups.
