🌊 WHERE THE TAPES COME BACK

HARROW POINT, MAINE

Every Late Returns tape crosses the counter at Neon Coffin Video in Harrow Point, a fog-bound town on the southern Maine coast. The Night Manager grew up near Boston and has kept the late shift here longer than anyone can remember.

  • THE AMUSEMENT PIERIt burned once. Some nights the music still plays after midnight.
  • WIDOW’S RISE BURIAL GROUNDThe old cemetery on the hill, looking down over the town and the dark water.
  • LOW TIDE MORTUARYThe town’s old mortuary, where the coast road bends away from the water.
