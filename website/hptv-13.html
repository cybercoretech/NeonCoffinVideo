HPTV 13

A local signal from the edge of the map.

Harrow Point Television began broadcasting in 1964 from a low concrete building above the seawall. It has one employee on record, no listed camera crew, and a nightly signal that reaches places beyond its broadcast radius.

ON AIR SINCE 1964 / HOST

MARLOW VANE

Marlow Vane is the only known employee of WHP-TV. His polished mid-Atlantic broadcast diction never shifts, and the same face appears in every surviving publicity still. He introduces films with perfect courtesy, even when the picture has already begun.

His weather reports are always accurate for Harrow Point—and nowhere else. He never turns to acknowledge the figures behind the cameras.

NO HUMAN CREW ON PAYROLL

THE BLACKBACKS

Tall, hooded figures appear in studio glass and old film reels. Their faces disappear wherever light falls. Camera motors start before the figures move; the sound reaches the control room seconds before the image.

Vane thanks them at the end of every broadcast without looking back. On the oldest surviving tape, one is already waiting in the empty studio.
