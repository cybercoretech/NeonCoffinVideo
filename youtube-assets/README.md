Banner text and logo sit within YouTube's recommended central safe area; the artwork is an original illustrated haunted Maine amusement pier scene.

To apply the graphics, open YouTube Studio → Customization → Profile. Upload the banner and profile picture, preview the crops, and publish. The watermark can be set from the same branding area. YouTube recommends 2560 × 1440 for banners, with a 1235 × 338 safe area at minimum upload dimensions; banner uploads must be 6 MB or smaller.

Official guidance: [Manage channel branding](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525) and [Channel banner and profile picture tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12950272).
